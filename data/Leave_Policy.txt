LEAVE POLICY

1. Casual Leave

Employees are allowed 12 casual leaves per year.

Question: How many casual leaves are allowed per year?
Answer: Employees are allowed 12 casual leaves per year.

Question: How many casual leaves are allowed?
Answer: 12 casual leaves are allowed per year.

Question: What is the annual casual leave entitlement?
Answer: The annual casual leave entitlement is 12 days.

Question: How many days of casual leave can an employee take in a year?
Answer: An employee can take 12 casual leaves in a year.


2. Leave Application

Employees must apply for leave through the HR portal.

Question: How should employees apply for leave?
Answer: Employees must apply for leave through the HR portal.

Question: Where should employees submit their leave request?
Answer: Employees should submit their leave request through the HR portal.

Question: How can an employee request leave?
Answer: An employee can request leave through the HR portal.


3. Sick Leave

Sick leave requires proper approval from the manager.

Question: Who must approve sick leave?
Answer: Sick leave requires proper approval from the manager.

Question: What is required for sick leave?
Answer: Proper approval from the manager is required for sick leave.

Question: Does sick leave require manager approval?
Answer: Yes. Sick leave requires proper approval from the manager.


4. Summary

Casual Leave:
Employees are allowed 12 casual leaves per year.

Leave Application:
Employees must apply for leave through the HR portal.

Sick Leave:
Sick leave requires proper approval from the manager.
