COMPANY HR POLICY

1. Purpose

The Company HR Policy provides guidelines for employees regarding attendance, working hours, leave, workplace conduct, performance, confidentiality, and employee responsibilities.

2. Working Hours

The standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

Employees are expected to report to work on time and remain available during their scheduled working hours.

3. Attendance

Employees must maintain regular attendance.

If an employee is unable to attend work, they should inform their manager as soon as possible.

Planned absences should be communicated to the manager in advance.

4. Casual Leave

Employees are allowed 12 casual leaves per year.

Casual leave can be used for personal work or short-term personal requirements.

Employees must submit casual leave requests through the HR portal.

5. Sick Leave

Employees may take sick leave when they are unwell.

Sick leave requires proper approval from the manager.

Employees should inform their manager as soon as possible when they cannot attend work due to illness.

6. Leave Application

All leave requests should be submitted through the HR portal.

Employees should select the appropriate leave type and provide the required dates.

Employees should wait for approval before assuming that their leave has been granted.

7. Manager Approval

Managers are responsible for reviewing employee leave requests.

Leave approval depends on business requirements, team availability, and company procedures.

A manager may approve or reject a leave request.

8. Remote Work

Employees working remotely must remain available during their scheduled working hours.

Employees must attend scheduled meetings and complete assigned work on time.

9. Workplace Conduct

Employees must maintain professional and respectful behavior.

Harassment, bullying, discrimination, and inappropriate workplace behavior are not permitted.

Employees should communicate professionally with colleagues, customers, and business partners.

10. Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential company information.

Company documents, customer information, employee information, passwords, and internal business information must not be shared with unauthorized individuals.

11. Information Security

Employees must use strong passwords and must not share passwords with others.

Employees should lock their computers when away from their workstation.

Suspicious emails, links, or security incidents should be reported to the appropriate company contact.

12. Performance

Employee performance may be reviewed periodically.

Employees are expected to complete assigned responsibilities, meet deadlines, and continuously improve their skills.

13. Training

Employees may participate in training programs to improve their technical, professional, and communication skills.

14. Employee Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for:

- Following company policies.
- Maintaining professional behavior.
- Protecting confidential information.
- Maintaining regular attendance.
- Completing assigned responsibilities.
- Following information security procedures.
- Submitting leave requests through the HR portal.

15. Policy Updates

The company may update HR policies when required.

Employees are responsible for reviewing and following the latest version of the company policies.
