EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Company Overview

Our company is committed to creating a professional, respectful, and productive workplace. Every employee is expected to follow company policies and maintain professional behavior.

Working Hours

The standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

Employees should be punctual and should inform their manager if they expect to be late or absent.

Attendance

Employees are expected to maintain regular attendance. Any planned absence should be communicated to the manager in advance.

Employees must record their attendance according to the company's attendance procedure.

Workplace Conduct

Employees must treat colleagues, customers, and business partners with respect.

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and inappropriate workplace behavior are not permitted.

Employees should maintain professional communication in emails, meetings, and other company communication channels.

Remote Work

Employees working remotely must remain available during their scheduled working hours.

Employees are expected to attend scheduled meetings and complete assigned work on time.

Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential company information.

Company documents, customer information, employee information, passwords, and internal business information must not be shared with unauthorized people.

Information Security

Employees should use strong passwords and must not share their passwords with others.

Employees should lock their computers when away from their workstation.

Suspicious emails, links, or security incidents should be reported to the appropriate company contact.

Performance

Employee performance may be reviewed periodically.

Employees are expected to complete their responsibilities, meet deadlines, and continuously improve their skills.

Training

Employees may participate in training programs to improve technical, professional, and communication skills.

Leave

Employees can request leave according to the company's Leave Policy.

Leave requests should be submitted through the HR portal and approved according to company procedures.

Employee Responsibilities

Every employee is responsible for following company policies, protecting company information, maintaining professional behavior, and completing assigned responsibilities.

Policy Changes

The company may update policies when required. Employees are responsible for reviewing and following the latest version of company policies.
